# Contact atlas — known physical families versus unproved completeness

This is an **organizing table, not a classification theorem**. A family's cost identity can be exact while the assertion that it is active, exhaustive or optimal remains unproved.

Use light coordinates U=t+x and W=t−x. The ordered signed Princess CDF F(t,x) must be defined on one legal spacetime domain; endpoint atoms and chronological first contact are part of the geometry. After an actual center crossing, keep **both** left and right surviving records.

## Obligatory junctions

**J1 — initial field:** reconcile inward I, moving-front P, endpoint S/J and shadow constraints under the same transport inequality and endpoint mass budget. A local contact equation does not automatically integrate to a legal global CDF.

**J2 — exit geometry:** price every precenter outgoing boundary, especially the W=0 interior ray and incoming-front terminal, without falsely replacing it by a center state. Couple to an outer function of both survivor records.

**J3 — center renewal:** prove a qualifying record gap on all reachable dangerous states and use the fixed field's central continuity modulus, or supply an **all-count** renewal potential. A bounded density is unnecessary for the first route. Endpoint mixing gives a value-dense finite-postcenter class member by member, with no uniform limiting count. Near-optimality alone does not remove the fully uncaught center exception.

**J4 — physical dual:** the [synchronized matching normal form](../12-global-closure/SYNCHRONIZED_PRESSURE_AND_FINITE_SUPPORT.md) permits equal-time matching and exact positive-time antipodal cancellation while retaining physical causal resources. A supplied fully matched N-path law has a finite polygonal realization; finite optimal support remains unproved. A matching upper must still be constructed, and a clock-only / Eulerian relaxation remains unsound.

The [compensated exchange](../12-global-closure/COMPENSATED_CURVATURE_AND_CUSP_RESPONSE.md) enlarges the local curvature class and proves finite global response for the atom-free front-one cusp. Its whole-inner criterion is still excluded through U=21/16, and it does not remove the phase-attachment gate.

## Disproof fixtures to preserve

- The [direct front-one projection](../12-global-closure/FRONT_ONE_PROJECTION_SECURITY_LOSS.md) lowers exact free-start security from 47/40 to 469/400. The loss persists for some bounded-density regularizations; this is not a high-security or value-class exclusion.
- A retained physical attack has **a=-7/8**, **b=67/64>1**: search domains restricted to positive starts or turns at b≤1 are not representative.
- A boundary shadow path violates loop-only proposed repairs: see the exact [boundary regression](../../packages/interval_global_closure/verify_boundary_shadow_kernel.py).
- A time-shifted prefix can lose contact at a later front: [prefix-compression diagnostic](../12-global-closure/INITIAL_PREFIX_COMPRESSION_DIAGNOSTIC.md).
- Finite initial support cannot attain V: [global nonattainment theorem](../12-global-closure/FINITE_INITIAL_SUPPORT_NONATTAINMENT.md).
- The front-one all-positive common-contact ansatz has a conditional exclusion and non-Puiseux endpoint obstruction: [front-one analysis](../12-global-closure/FRONT_ONE_CONTACT_NONALGEBRAICITY.md).
- Monotonicity of F_U in both light coordinates cannot persist throughout the inner triangle through U=41/40: the [endpoint-sweep bound](../12-global-closure/POSITIVE_PREFRONT_RESPONSE_AND_MODULUS.md) is below 1137/800, without any front-completion hypothesis.
- A [static majority-center-atom law](../12-global-closure/GLOBAL_FREE_START_ZENO_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md) forces every optimal free-start Monster to reverse infinitely often near zero. It has low security and does not exclude an equilibrium-specific normal form.

**Required next addition to this atlas:** for each proposed active sheet, specify its parameter domain, endpoint/center hitting chronology, exact cost functional, equal-cost or inequality status, transport trace, and a proof or counterexample for paths crossing from adjacent sheets. Until then this is a diagnostic ledger, not a finite solution.
